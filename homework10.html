Zoo After Hours

Noah is the last zookeeper on site. The zoo is closed. Something is still awake.

Noah finishes his night shift routine the way he always does: one last check, one last lock, one last “okay, we’re good.” The zoo feels empty in that after-hours way—like the world is holding its breath.

Then a sound snaps through the quiet. Metal… or claws… or something scraping where it shouldn’t be. Noah pauses with his keys in hand, suddenly aware of how loud his own breathing is.

Two hallways split ahead. Left looks colder. Right sounds… fast.

His radio crackles    He keeps moving

The radio hisses with static like it’s chewing on a message. Noah taps it once. Twice. For a second it sounds like a word—then it dissolves back into noise.

The worst part is what follows: silence. Like the zoo is listening back.

He checks the flashlight    He pockets the radio

The flashlight beam flickers. Not dead. Just tired. Noah rolls it in his palm like he can convince it to behave.

He turns it off again to save battery… and the hallway instantly feels longer than it did two seconds ago. Jejeje—of course this happens on his shift.

He moves anyway

Two hallways. Two different vibes. Left smells like cold concrete and damp air. Right smells like fruit and warm hay.

Somewhere above him, something taps… tap-tap… like it’s pacing.

Left hall    Right hall

The left hall is colder. Noah’s footsteps sound louder than they should, like the floor is trying to snitch on him. He tells himself it’s fine. It’s always fine.

Then he sees it: a painted gate at the end of the hall. The color looks brighter than it should in the dark, almost like it’s glowing.

He slows down    He walks like it’s normal

He slows down. Silence presses in around him. For one second he hears breathing that isn’t his… then it stops, like whatever it is realized he noticed.

The gate is right there now. The paint is too clean. The latch looks recently touched.

The BLUE gate

He keeps walking like it’s normal. Like he’s not in a closed zoo at night hearing mystery sounds. Like he’s not gripping his keys hard enough to leave dents in his palm.

The gate waits anyway. Calm. Patient. Like it already knows he’s coming.

The BLUE gate

The latch clicks shut behind Noah. The air inside feels… occupied. Not moving, but there.

His fingers find a switch on the wall. He doesn’t love how easy it is to find.

Lights on    Lights off

Darkness. Immediate. Noah can’t see anything, but he can feel the space shift like it got smaller.

Two glowing eyes appear—steady, low, confident. Noah reaches forward like an idiot (respectfully)… and his hands meet thick fur.

roarrr rrrrrr

The sound grows inside the dark until it feels like the air itself is shaking.

He flips the switch (lights back ON)    Back

The lights snap on. A lion stands close enough to fill Noah’s vision—eyes locked, face calm, like this is just another Tuesday.

Noah’s brain does that thing where it gives him two thoughts at once: don’t move and MOVE RIGHT NOW.

The lion’s tail flicks once. Slow. Controlled.

Noah shifts his weight    Noah holds still

He shifts his weight and the lion’s head tilts—tiny movement, huge meaning. Noah feels his heart hit his ribs like it’s trying to escape without him.

He runs    He stays

He holds still like a statue. The lion takes one slow step forward—no rush, no panic—just power.

Noah realizes “still” can be the wrong choice, too.

He runs    He stays

The right hall is warmer. Quick taps echo above him like something is sprinting along the bars. Noah looks up and catches a blur—too fast to be a person, too intentional to be “just the wind.”

A painted gate waits at the end. The color looks playful… which honestly makes it more suspicious.

He follows the tapping    He follows the smell of fruit

He follows the tapping. It circles above him like it’s showing off. Then—jingle. Like a keychain.

Noah checks his pocket automatically. Still there… for now.

The PINK gate

He follows the fruit smell. Sweet, warm, and way too strong for this hour. Something brushes the top of his hair like a mischievous breeze.

Okay. Not cute. Not cute at all.

The PINK gate

Something swoops past overhead. A small screech. A key-jingle. Noah looks down—his keys are gone.

Of course. Of course the keys are gone. Why wouldn’t they be?

Lights off    Lights on

Lights off. The enclosure becomes a maze of shadows. Something small moves close to the ground, then above his head, then behind him—like it’s testing how fast he can turn.

The key-jingle circles him… left, right, above… like a tiny victory parade.

He flips the switch (lights back ON)    Back

The lights come on. A monkey swings by with Noah’s keys like a trophy, pauses mid-swing, and stares at him like it’s judging his whole personality.

The monkey squeaks—almost like laughter—and swings lower, just close enough to make Noah think he could maybe get the keys back… maybe.

Noah’s next move changes the vibe instantly.

He plays along    He bolts

Noah raises his hands slowly like, “okay okay, you win.” The monkey squeaks again and swings in a circle, showing off like it’s performing for an audience.

Noah grabs a soft enrichment toy off the side (thank you, training manuals) and tosses it gently. The monkey catches it like it’s the best thing that’s ever happened.

The monkey drops the keys

Noah bolts. The monkey screeches—not playful anymore—and the jingle gets faster behind him like tiny footsteps on a drum.

Noah doesn’t look back. He just runs until the air changes and the zoo feels open again.

He makes it out

Noah makes it out. The keys are still in his hand. His laugh comes out shaky, like his body is trying to pretend this was funny.

The zoo goes quiet again—like nothing happened. Like it didn’t just happen to him.

SAFE + KEYS ✅

Again

Noah gets out, breathing hard. Behind him, a tiny victory screech echoes like a laugh track from a horror-comedy.

The keys do not return. Tomorrow is going to be… embarrassing.

SAFE… NO KEYS 😬

Again

Noah hesitates. The lion doesn’t. The flashlight hits the floor, the light spins, and the zoo becomes a blur of shadow and breath.

The night shift ends here.
