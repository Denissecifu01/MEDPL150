Zoo After Hours

Noah is the last zookeeper on site. The zoo is closed. Something is still awake.

Noah finishes his routine the way he always does: one last check, one last lock, one last “okay, we’re good.” The zoo feels empty in that after-hours way—like the world is holding its breath.

Then a sound snaps through the quiet. Metal… or claws… or something scraping where it shouldn’t be. Noah pauses with his keys in hand, suddenly aware of how loud his own breathing is.

Two hallways split ahead.

The radio    The split

The radio hisses with static like it’s chewing on a message. Noah taps it once. Twice. For a second it sounds like a word and then it dissolves back into noise.

The worst part is what follows: silence. Like the zoo is listening back.

The flashlight    The split

The flashlight beam flickers. Not dead, just tired. Noah turns it off again to save battery and instantly regrets it.

Jejeje… of course this happens on his shift.

Forward

Left smells like cold concrete. Right smells like fruit and warm hay. Somewhere above, something taps—tap-tap—like it’s pacing.

Left    Right

The left hall is colder. Noah’s footsteps sound louder than they should, like the floor is trying to snitch.

A painted gate waits at the end,too clean, too bright.

The BLUE gate

The latch clicks shut behind Noah. The air inside feels… occupied. His fingers find a switch on the wall.

Lights on    Lights off

Darkness. Immediate. The space feels smaller. Two glowing eyes appear very steady, low, confident.

Noah reaches forward and his hands meet thick fur.

roarrr rrrrrr

His fingers remember exactly where the switch is.

The switch (lights back ON)    Back

The lights snap on. A lion stands close enough to fill Noah’s vision eyes locked, face calm, like this is normal.

Noah’s brain gives him two thoughts at once: don’t move and MOVE RIGHT NOW. The lion’s tail flicks once. Slow. Controlled.

A shift    A freeze

Noah shifts his weight and the lion’s head tilts tiny movement, huge meaning. His heart hits his ribs like it’s trying to leave first.

Run    Stay

Noah holds still. The lion takes one slow step forward with no rush, no panic at all, just power.

“Still” can be the wrong choice too.

Run    Stay

The right hall is warmer. Quick taps echo above him like something sprinting along the bars. A painted gate waits,with a suspicious energy.

The PINK gate

Something swoops overhead. A small screech. A key jingle. Noah looks down and realizes his keys are gone.

Lights off    Lights on

Lights off. The enclosure becomes a maze of shadows. Something small moves close to the ground, then above his head, then behind him.

The key-jingle circles him like a tiny victory parade.

The switch (lights back ON)    Back

The lights come on. A monkey swings by, with Noah’s keys like a trophy, pauses mid-swing, and stares like it’s judging his whole personality.

It swings lower and close enough to make him think he might get the keys back… maybe.

Play    Run

Noah raises his hands and says “okay okay, you win.” He tosses a soft enrichment toy gently. The monkey catches it like it’s the best thing that’s ever happened.

A second later, the keys drop—right at Noah’s feet, like a mic drop.

Out

Noah bolts. The screech behind him changesless playful. The jingle gets faster like tiny footsteps on a drum.

Out

Noah makes it out. The keys are still in his hand. His laugh comes out shaky, like his body is trying to pretend this was funny.

SAFE + KEYS ✅

Again

Noah gets out, breathing hard. Somewhere behind him, a tiny victory screech echoes. The keys do not return.

SAFE… NO KEYS 😬

Again

Noah hesitates. The lion doesn’t. The flashlight hits the floor, the light spins, and the night ends.
